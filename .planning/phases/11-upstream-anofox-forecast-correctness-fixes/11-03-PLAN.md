<objective>
Make the Standard Theta Model estimate its smoothing parameter the way R `forecast::thetaf`
does, and make its drift term and prediction intervals follow the thetaf formulas, removing the
random-walk bias — delivered as branch fix/theta-alpha-intervals with an open PR (D-01, D-02,
D-03, D-09).

Purpose: UPST-04 (audit H6, H7 "Theta 2x at h>=6"). `Theta::new()` hard-codes a fixed
alpha of 0.1 with optimisation off; its SSE ignores the initial level; its drift uses 1/alpha
instead of (1 - (1 - alpha)^n)/alpha; its interval factor is an AR(1)-style sum instead of
1 + alpha^2 (h - 1). Open question Q1 from research is resolved: AutoTheta builds its STM
candidate from `Theta::new()` / `Theta::seasonal_with_decomposition()` (auto.rs ~184-190) and its
OTM candidate from the separate `OptimizedTheta` (optimises alpha AND theta), so fixing the
constructors propagates and STM/OTM stay distinct. The extension's Phase 10 pinning test
(theta_croston_fitted.rs) will need updating in Phase 17 — note it in the SUMMARY.
Output: corrected theta/model.rs, doc fixes, R generator + fixture, reference tests, CHANGELOG,
pushed branch, open PR.
</objective>
