<objective>
Route exogenous forecasting through the crate's own exog support (D-10): attach regressors to
the crate TimeSeries, fit the requested model itself (AutoARIMA order search with regressors,
Optimized/Dynamic/Auto Theta, ETS, ARIMA orders, MFLES, MSTL), predict with
`predict_with_exog_intervals`, give regressors constant in history a 0 effect, pass regressor
NULLs via validity (D-12, FCST-08), delete the wrapper OLS + fake-model-on-residuals code, and
make the SQL exog surface take params and emit NULL-safe lists.

Purpose: FCST-05, regressor half of FCST-08, and the SQL-surface NULL handling of FCST-01/03.
Output: rewritten forecast_with_exog, FFI validity fields, C++ marshalling, tests, DEP-01 SQL re-run.

Planner decisions (Claude's discretion, recorded): MSTLForecaster's exog support (new in 0.15.10)
is routed like the other exog-capable models (orchestrator decision); a non-exog model given
regressors errors instead of silently ignoring them (D-04 spirit); regressors constant in
history are dropped before the crate OLS (0 effect by construction, avoids a singular
normal-equation system); the wrapper OLS path is deleted, so no wrapper-side OLS coefficient
filtering remains (D-10's "if a wrapper OLS path remains" clause no longer applies). The
anofox-regression dependency becomes unused by anofox-fcst-core; it stays declared (removal
would touch Cargo.lock and the Phase 9 snapshot bless helper) and is recorded as a follow-up in
the SUMMARY.
</objective>
