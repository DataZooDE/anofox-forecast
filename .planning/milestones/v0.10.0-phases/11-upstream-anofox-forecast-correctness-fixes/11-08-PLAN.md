Hyndman et al. (2008) interval footing as ETS by routing them through `ets_interval_bounds`, then

are reachable from the extension (ModelType SES/Holt/HoltWinters/SeasonalES) and Phase 17 FCST-09
must match their intervals against R ses/holt/hw — fixing them in this branch avoids a second
upstream release cycle.
Output: four ES-family files routed through the helper, a reference test, CHANGELOG, PR ready.
