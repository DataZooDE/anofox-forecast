<objective>
Fix AutoARIMA's differencing choice and its candidate scoring so that it behaves like R
`forecast::auto.arima` (Hyndman–Khandakar): D by the seasonal-strength test, d by repeated KPSS on
the seasonally differenced series, and candidates ranked by AICc on a common likelihood basis —
first half of branch fix/auto-arima-order-selection (D-01, D-03, D-06). Plan 11-05 finishes the
search (R default bounds, HK stepwise) and readies the PR.

Purpose: UPST-01 (audit H3): random walks select (0,1,0) 1% of the time (R 69.5%) and stationary
AR(0.7) gets d >= 1 98% of the time (R 19%). Root causes: d from a variance-ratio heuristic
(diff.rs suggest_differencing), D from a second, different variance-ratio heuristic
(auto_arima.rs suggest_seasonal_differencing) inconsistent with the STL strength test used for
the seasonal fallback, the plain-ARIMA scorer comparing (0,0) on the full sample against p+q > 0 on
a trimmed sample with no mean term, and the SARIMA scorer omitting the AICc correction. Per
research Pattern 3, d-selection uses its own KPSS statistic and critical-value interpolation (not
`validation::kpss_test`'s p-value), so this branch has no dependency on fix/adf-kpss-correctness.
Output: corrected diff.rs / auto_arima.rs / model.rs, R generator + fixture, reference tests,
pushed branch, draft PR.
</objective>
